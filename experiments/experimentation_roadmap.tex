\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Short record of approaches we tried for video-to-haptic ground-truth generation,
their outcomes, and whether we changed approach or continued.
The detector finds start, peak, and end on its own (no hardcoded times).
Grading marks in the notebook score a run; they never steer detection.
\end{abstract}

\section{Goal}
From a short video, make four vibration sound files (algorithms A--D) so people can feel and rate them.
We want continuous flow (like thunder rumble) plus clear hits (like blasts), similar to a dense hand-made reference map, not only sparse spikes.

Hard cases that keep driving the work:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
  \item Mixed clips: cannon volley, then a distant tank drive, then a close car rumble in one file.
  \item Intermittent car rumble: vibrate on bursts, stay quiet in the gaps. No fake bang accents on rumble.
  \item Engine-start swell and camera-angle loudness changes that are still the same drive.
\end{itemize}

\section{Current stack}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
  \item Pull mono sound from the video (44.1\,kHz). Video classification is parked (\texttt{use\_video: false}).
  \item Frame-level sound event detection (SED): PANNs when installed, otherwise dense AST windows. Decode with median filter + hysteresis, then split volleys at posterior peaks.
  \item Snap bangs to spectral-flux attacks. Promote missed shots from flux (anchor + volley rules). Demote rumble-like false bangs.
  \item Gate vehicle rumble on loud RMS islands, with one loudness floor \emph{per scene} so a close car cannot hide a distant drive. Repeated quiet stretches are treated as rhythm (gaps stay quiet); one long quiet stretch is treated as a camera angle (kept).
  \item Write \texttt{events.json} (includes a \texttt{sustained\_gate} report: floor per scene, dropped spans) and \texttt{gated\_audio.wav}.
  \item Run Sound2Hap A--D: quiet continuous vibration underneath (bed) plus stronger short hits on top (accents, 25\,ms lead, 45\,ms release between volley hits). Rumble spans follow source loudness and rhythm. The four algorithms themselves are unchanged.
\end{enumerate}

Scoring (notebook, after generation): \texttt{TRUTH\_SHOT\_TIMES} / \texttt{TRUTH\_RUMBLE\_TIMES} are a grading key for this clip only. Shots get DCASE-style onset-collar F1; rumble gets span coverage. Empty lists mean ``nothing to score'', not a detector failure.

\section{Terms}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
  \item Fixed list of event types we care about (taxonomy).
  \item Model that links images, audio, and text in one shared space (ImageBind).
  \item Finds objects in each video frame with boxes (YOLO).
  \item Window tagging: classify $\sim$1\,s chunks, so onsets land on a coarse grid.
  \item Frame-level SED: a score every 10--100\,ms, decoded into start/end like DCASE.
  \item Break one long blob of sound into separate bangs at each loud peak (peak split).
  \item Snap the event start to the exact moment the bang begins (onset refine / spectral flux).
  \item Promote a flux attack that the classifier missed, if it matches this clip's blast level (impulsive promote; volley mode relaxes the rule near a confirmed blast).
  \item Keep audio only at the times of selected event types (temporal + category gate).
  \item Only pass short sharp bangs, ignore long rumble (impulsive gate).
  \item Loudness floor for rumble: keep frames above a threshold measured on active (non-silent) audio (salience).
  \item Scene: a stretch of the clip with its own recording level (cut-together sources). Each scene gets its own floor.
  \item RMS island: a contiguous loud run that becomes one haptic rumble window.
  \item How many real events we find versus miss (detection recall).
  \item Event-based F1: one-to-one onset match inside a collar (DCASE Task~4 uses 200\,ms).
  \item Coverage: for rumble, fraction of hand marks that fall inside a detected span (marks are moments in a burst, not onsets).
  \item Automatically boost quiet parts so they are not ignored (AGC level).
  \item Quiet continuous vibration that runs under the whole clip (bed).
  \item Mute the quietest share of the track so real silence stays silent (percentile silence).
  \item Squeeze the gap between loud and quiet so soft rumble still feels (envelope-exponent compression).
  \item Remember recent loud levels so a bang does not over-boost the quiet parts next to it (peak hold).
  \item Scale the quiet continuous layer to a target strength (normalize bed).
  \item Strong short hits placed on top of the quiet layer (accents).
  \item Place the accent slightly before the audio attack so a slow actuator is felt on time (accent lead).
  \item Taper the previous bang before the next lands, so a volley reads as separate hits (accent release).
  \item Phone code that turns WAV loudness into vibration strength (parser).
\end{itemize}

\section{Where a combined clip stood (run 28) vs the fixture now}
User clip: first $\sim$6--8\,s cannons, 10--18\,s tank drive, then car rumble.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Cannons:} seven shots at the right times; drive clanks were not promoted to explosions. Timing of peaks was within $\sim$10\,ms of the attacks; remaining ``delay'' on the phone is actuator rise time (25\,ms lead kept).
  \item \textbf{Drive:} missed. One clip-wide floor (0.63) sat above the distant drive.
  \item \textbf{Car:} two spans; 21.7--27.8\,s glued three bursts into one 6\,s buzz (too sensitive / no rhythm).
  \item \textbf{Scores:} invalid --- grading key was still the previous video.
\end{itemize}

On the synthetic combined-clip fixture after the scene + rhythm + rendering changes: drive 10--18\,s as one span (100\% coverage), car as four bursts of 1.4--2.0\,s with silent gaps, drive RMS clearly below car bursts across A--D.

\section{Key takeaways}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
  \item Sound2Hap A--D stay as ports of the official scripts. We change context filtering, stitching, and level shaping instead of rewriting the algorithms.
  \item Finding event times alone is not enough, because vibration that only fires on detected events feels sparse next to a dense hand-made reference. Quiet continuous vibration (bed) plus stronger short hits (accents) is needed for thunder and rumble.
  \item Window tagging is too coarse for volleys. Frame-level SED plus flux refine/promote is the detector; video is parked until the visual model actually speaks acoustic categories.
  \item A mixed clip cannot share one loudness floor. Measure rumble per scene, then decide steady vs rhythmic from repeated gaps, not from percentiles alone.
  \item Rumble power and rhythm live in the renderer: do not flatten dynamics, do not peak-normalize every span, do not let one bang ring into the next.
  \item Grading marks must be for \emph{this} clip and must not feed the detector. Empty marks mean nothing to score; a miss with nothing in \texttt{sustained\_gate.dropped} means the classifier never proposed it.
\end{itemize}
